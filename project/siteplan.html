SMART HOUSE

Website Site Plan

Site Name

SMART HOUSE

This name represents a modern technology and accessories store based in Benin, West Africa. The name "Smart House" conveys innovation, technology, and quality products for the home and personal use.

Site Purpose

The SMART HOUSE website provides an online presence for the store, showcasing available products including Bluetooth accessories, phone accessories, perfumes, and small appliances. The site will allow customers to discover products, learn about the store, and get in contact with the business.

Scenarios

  • What types of Bluetooth accessories does SMART HOUSE offer and what are the prices?
  • Where is the SMART HOUSE store located and what are the opening hours?
  • How can I contact SMART HOUSE to place an order or ask a question?
